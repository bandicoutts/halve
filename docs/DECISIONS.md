- **2026-06-02 · M-5: Double-tap-to-exit guard on Android back gesture.** On Android 13+ (API 33+) with target SDK 36 (Flutter default), the predictive-back system (`OnBackInvokedDispatcher`) bypasses Flutter's `popSystemNavigator()` and all Dart-level interception (`PopScope` on `StatefulShellRoute` branch roots, `BackButtonListener`, custom `RootBackButtonDispatcher`, `WidgetsBindingObserver.didPopRoute()`) when go_router has nothing to pop — Flutter unregisters its own callback and the OS closes the activity directly. Fix: `MainActivity.kt` registers its own `OnBackInvokedCallback` at `PRIORITY_DEFAULT` *before* `super.onCreate()` so Flutter's callback (registered in `onResume()`) has higher LIFO priority and fires first. When Flutter unregisters (nothing to pop), ours becomes the active callback: first press shows a "Press back again to exit" `Toast` and starts a 2-second window; second press within the window calls `finish()`; after 2 s the window resets. Also upgraded go_router from 14.8.1 → 17.2.3 (no breaking changes for this codebase — all paths are lowercase). Verified on emulator: first press stays open, second within 2 s exits, second after 2 s resets toast, Settings back navigates normally without triggering the guard.

- **2026-06-02 · M-1 + M-3: Fixed archive month navigation and added pull-to-refresh.** Root cause of M-1: `PuzzleRepository.fetchArchive` called `.order('date')` without `ascending: true`. The PostgREST Flutter client defaults to **descending** order, so `days.first` was the newest puzzle (today) rather than the oldest. `_deriveMonths` used `days.first.date` to derive the oldest available month — always the current month — making `prevMonth(view, oldest)` always null and the `<` chevron permanently disabled. Fix: added `ascending: true` explicitly. For M-3: wrapped the archive `ListView` with `RefreshIndicator.adaptive` (with `AlwaysScrollableScrollPhysics` so the indicator fires even when the calendar fits on one screen); `onRefresh` calls `ref.refresh(archiveDataProvider.future)`. Also updated `seed.sql` with past-month puzzle fixtures (intervals from current date) so local-dev runs can exercise month navigation without production data.

- **2026-06-02 · Notification scheduling falls back from exact to inexact alarms.** `scheduleDaily()` called `AndroidScheduleMode.exactAllowWhileIdle` unconditionally. On Android 12+ without `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` permission (emulators, most devices until user grants it) this throws an unhandled `PlatformException` before `runApp()` completes, crashing the app on launch. Fix: wrap in try/catch; on failure retry with `inexactAllowWhileIdle`. Inexact alarms fire within ~15 min of the target time — acceptable for a daily puzzle reminder. The permission can still be requested later to upgrade to exact scheduling if needed.
