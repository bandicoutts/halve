## Google Play

**App name:** Halve — Daily Logic Puzzle

**Short description (80 chars):**
One daily logic puzzle. Pure deduction, no luck.

**Full description (4000 chars):**
Halve is a daily logic puzzle.

Each day, a new 6×6 grid appears. Fill it with two symbols — a solid disc and a ring — following three rules.

Every row and column must contain exactly three of each symbol. No three of the same symbol can appear consecutively in a row or column. Some cells share an edge marked = or ≠, telling you whether those two cells must match or differ.

There is always exactly one solution. Every answer can be reached by logic alone — no guessing.

**What's in the app**

· One new puzzle every day
· An archive of every past puzzle — catch up on any you've missed
· Unlimited practice puzzles at easy, medium, or hard difficulty
· A streak counter and personal stats (sign in to keep them safe)
· No ads, no timers, no pressure

**How it works**

Tap a cell to cycle it through empty, disc, and ring. Fill the grid following the three constraints. When you're stuck, use the hint — it reveals one cell without spoiling the rest. Undo if you change your mind.

The rules take about two minutes to learn. The puzzles get interesting quickly.
